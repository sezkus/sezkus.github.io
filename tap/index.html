Privacy Policy & Support · Last updated 23 September 2026

Privacy Policy

Tap does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics services, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What the app stores

Everything Tap remembers stays in the app's own storage on your device:

  • your streak count and the date of your last tap;
  • the words you have collected and any notes you wrote about them;
  • your settings: language, vibration, reminder time, streak saves;
  • how many daily taps you have made, which decides when the free days end;
  • whether your yearly membership is active and when it renews, so the app can open without waiting for the store.

On iPhone, when you are signed in to iCloud, the same records are also kept in your own iCloud account (the iCloud key-value store), so your collection comes back after reinstalling the app or on a new iPhone. That copy belongs to your Apple Account; Tap has no server and cannot see it. Signing out of iCloud, or turning iCloud off for Tap in Settings, stops it.

Apart from that, none of this leaves your device. It is also included in your device's own encrypted backup (iCloud or Google) if you have backups turned on.

You can export your collection yourself at any time — as readable text, or as a backup file you can restore later — from the menu. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Notifications

If you set a reminder time, Tap schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and no push token is created.

Purchases

The yearly membership is sold and charged entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. Tap never sees your name, payment details, or store account.

Checking whether a membership is active is done by RevenueCat, which acts as our processor. When you subscribe, restore or renew, the store's receipt goes to RevenueCat together with an anonymous identifier it generates for this install, and it answers whether the membership is active. No name, email or payment detail is involved, and nothing identifies you as a person. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. The membership's terms are in the Terms of Use.

Ads for Tap

Tap shows no ads. We do advertise the app itself, and we measure which ad brought someone here so the budget is not spent blind. Nothing about your words, notes or streak is ever part of this.

  • Apple Search Ads. Apple gives the app a token that says an ad on the App Store led to this install. It contains no identifier for you, so no permission is needed.
  • Meta. Matching an install to an ad on Facebook or Instagram needs your device's advertising identifier. iOS asks you first, in Apple's own prompt, and Tap asks for nothing until you allow it. If you decline — or never see the prompt — this simply does not happen, and the app is unchanged.

Both are handled through RevenueCat, which matches its own purchase records to the ad network. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Tap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
