Reklamları sunmak için Google ve reklam iş ortakları cihazınızdan şu verileri toplayabilir:

  • Reklam kimliği ve cihaz tanımlayıcıları: iOS'ta reklam kimliği (IDFA) yalnızca takip izni verirseniz; Android'de Android reklam kimliği ve uygulama grubu kimliği (app set ID) gibi tanımlayıcılar.

  • Cihaz bilgileri: cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil ve ekran boyutu gibi.
  • Reklam ve uygulama etkileşimleri: gösterilen reklamlar, tıklamalar, video izlemeleri ve uygulamanın açılması gibi etkileşimler.
  • Tanı verileri: reklam yazılımının çökme, performans (örneğin açılış süresi) ve diğer teknik tanı bilgileri.

Bu veriler reklam göstermek ve ölçmek, reklam dolandırıcılığını önlemek, analiz yapmak ve izin verdiyseniz reklamları kişiselleştirmek için kullanılır. Satın alma yapmasanız da uygulama açılışta, fiyatları gösterebilmek ve satın alma durumunuzu öğrenmek için RevenueCat'e bağlanır; bu sırada anonim kimlik ve cihaz bilgileri gönderilir. Satın alma geçmişi yalnızca bir satın alma yaptıysanız oluşur.

7. Kullanım analitiği (Firebase Analytics)

Oyunu geliştirmek için, örneğin oyuncuların ertesi gün geri dönüp dönmediğini ve hangi seviyede takıldığını görmek için Google'ın Firebase Analytics hizmetini kullanırız. Firebase şu verileri toplar:

  • Uygulama örneği kimliği (app instance ID): uygulamanın bu kurulumu için rastgele oluşturulan bir kimlik. Uygulamayı silip yeniden yüklediğinizde değişir. Reklam kimliği (IDFA ya da Android reklam kimliği) analitik için kullanılmaz.
  • Kullanım bilgileri: uygulamanın ilk açılışı, oturumlar ve süreleri, geri dönüş (tutma) oranı.
  • Oyun olayları: bir seviyenin başlaması (`level_start`); bitmesi, aldığınız yıldız ve temizlik yüzdesi (`level_end`); yarıda bırakılması (`level_abandon`); "Reklamsız oyna" satın alması (`purchase_no_ads`) ve jeton paketi satın alması, yani hangi paket ve kaç jeton (`purchase_coins`). Firebase, mağaza satın almalarını ayrıca kendiliğinden kaydedebilir.
  • Cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri: cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, uygulama sürümü ve dil.
  • Yaklaşık konum: IP adresinden çıkarılan ülke (ve bölge ya da şehir) düzeyinde konum. Hassas konum toplanmaz.

Bu olaylara adınız, e-postanız ya da bir hesap bilgisi eklenmez. Firebase panelinde çoğunlukla toplu raporları görürüz (örneğin "ertesi gün geri dönen oyuncuların oranı"). Google bu verileri Firebase ve Google Analytics koşullarına göre işler ve Türkiye ile Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı dışındaki sunucularda (örneğin ABD'de) işleyebilir.

  • Google Gizlilik Politikası: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase'de gizlilik ve güvenlik: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers Cleanly, the game published for iOS and Android ("the app"). It explains which data is kept where when you use the app, which services collect which data, and what your rights are. The app has no accounts; you do not need to sign up or log in to play.

2. Data controller

  • Data controller: Sezer Kusku (individual developer)
  • Contact: hello.scannerstudio@gmail.com

Write to this address with any question or request about your personal data.

3. In short

  • Your game progress, stars, coins and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • As the developer we run no servers of our own and receive no personal data from you directly. In the services below we see only records tied to anonymous identifiers and aggregated reports.
  • The ads in the app are served by Google AdMob, which collects the device and usage data described in section 5.
  • In-app purchases are optional ("Play ad-free" and coin packs). Apple or Google takes the payment; your card and payment details never reach the app or us. RevenueCat validates purchases under an anonymous identifier (section 6).
  • We measure how the game is used (sessions, how many players come back, level progress) with Google's Firebase Analytics. It uses an anonymous app instance ID; the advertising identifier is not used for analytics (section 7).
  • Leaderboards are optional: Apple Game Center on iOS, Google Play Games on Android (section 8).
  • The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo permissions.

4. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only in your device's local storage:

  • Level progress: the stars and scores you earned on each level, unlocked levels, and the number of failed rounds in a row on a level.
  • Coins and looks: coin balance, total coins earned, daily and streak rewards already collected, and the looks you bought with coins (squeegee and view looks).
  • Lives: the number of lives and the time of the last refill.
  • Daily history: your best round of each day and your streak.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, language, the selected looks, and whether the tutorial has been shown.
  • Purchases: a flag showing that "Play ad-free" was bought, whether its 500 bonus coins were paid out, the amount of coins the store has confirmed but that have not yet been added to your wallet (pending coins), and the list of store transaction IDs already processed, so that the same coin pack is never added twice.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. If your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or Android backup) is turned on, the operating system may back up app data to your Apple or Google account as part of the device backup; we have no access to those backups.

Your ad consent choice is stored on your device by Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). Your answer to the iOS tracking prompt is stored by iOS itself. The RevenueCat (section 6) and Firebase (section 7) software also keep their own anonymous identifiers on your device.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, optional rewarded ads, and interstitial ads between levels at set intervals. Ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating. If you bought "Play ad-free", the banner and interstitial ads are not shown; rewarded ads remain available as an option.

To serve ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect the following from your device:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers: on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking; on Android identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

6. In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

The app offers two kinds of optional purchase:

  • Play ad-free: a one-time, permanent (non-consumable) purchase. It removes the banner and interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available as an option.
  • Coin packs: consumable products (400, 1,500 and 4,200 coins).

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) takes the payment. Your payment details, such as your card number or billing address, never reach the app or us; Apple or Google processes them under its own privacy policy.

To validate purchases the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat checks the store's purchase receipt on its own servers and tells the app what you own. For this, RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous app user ID: a random identifier created by RevenueCat. It is not linked to your name, your email or your Apple/Google account.
  • Device and operating system information: for example platform, operating system version, app version, language and store country.
  • Purchase history: which product you bought, the date, the price and currency, and the store's transaction ID and purchase receipt.
  • As with any internet connection, your IP address may also reach RevenueCat.

Even if you never buy anything, the app connects to RevenueCat on launch to show prices and to check what you own; the anonymous ID and device information are sent at that time. A purchase history exists only if you have made a purchase.

RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf (as a processor) and may process it on servers outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area (for example in the United States). As the developer we can see purchase records tied to the anonymous ID in the RevenueCat dashboard; these records do not identify you by name or account.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

To improve the game, for example to see whether players come back the next day and on which level they get stuck, we use Google's Firebase Analytics. Firebase collects:

  • The app instance ID: a random identifier created for this installation of the app. It changes if you uninstall and reinstall the app. The advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID) is not used for analytics.
  • Usage information: the app's first launch, sessions and their length, and retention (how many players come back).
  • Game events: a level starting (`level_start`); a level ending, with the stars and the clean percentage (`level_end`); a level being abandoned (`level_abandon`); buying "Play ad-free" (`purchase_no_ads`); and buying a coin pack, that is which pack and how many coins (`purchase_coins`). Firebase may also record store purchases automatically.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • Approximate location: country (and region or city) level, derived from the IP address. Precise location is not collected.

No name, email or account information is added to these events. In Firebase we mostly see aggregated reports (for example "the share of players who came back the next day"). Google processes this data under the Firebase and Google Analytics terms and may process it on servers outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area (for example in the United States).

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Privacy and security in Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

8. Leaderboards (optional)

If you are signed in to these services on your device, the app connects to Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android and submits scores to two leaderboards:

  • Daily board: the hidden score of your round on the day's puzzle. It is a number between 0 and 10000, not the clean percentage you see on screen.
  • Total stars: the sum of the stars you earned across all levels.

Scores are submitted together with your Game Center or Play Games player ID. On the boards, the display name you chose for that account (Game Center nickname or Play Games gamer name) and your score are visible to other players. Scores are stored on Apple's or Google's servers under their privacy policies; we see only what the boards show to everyone. If you are not signed in to these services, the app submits nothing and the game plays exactly the same.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

12. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Settings → Reset progress deletes levels, stars, coins, looks bought with coins, lives and the daily history (including the streak); your sound, haptics and language settings stay. Coins you bought are part of the balance, so they are deleted too; the "Play ad-free" purchase flag is not. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data (including the purchase flags and coins you bought); "Play ad-free" can be brought back with Settings → About → Restore purchases, coin packs cannot. If you restore a device backup, the data in that backup may come back.
  • Purchase records may be kept by RevenueCat for as long as we use the service and for as long as legal obligations such as tax or accounting require. If you want them deleted, write to us; to find the record we may need the order number from the store (the "Order ID" from Apple, or the number starting with "GPA." from Google Play). Your purchase history at Apple and Google follows their rules.
  • Analytics data follows the Google Analytics retention setting: event-level data is kept for 2 months by default (it can be extended to at most 14 months); aggregated reports may be kept longer. Uninstalling and reinstalling the app creates a new app instance ID; data tied to the old ID is deleted when the retention period ends.
  • Advertising data follows Google's retention rules: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. You can turn tracking off on iOS, and reset or delete your advertising ID on Android. Your ad settings at Google: https://myadcenter.google.com
  • Leaderboard data follows Apple's and Google's rules. You can delete data linked to your Apple Account at https://privacy.apple.com, and your Google Play Games data from your Play Games profile settings or your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com). Resetting progress does not delete scores that were already submitted.
